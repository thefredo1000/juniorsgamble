World map layout -- edit this grid and hand it back.

Every cell is one 16px step of the player. Coordinates are world pixels,
centered on the map (the player starts the game at world 0,0 today):

    world_x = -432 + (16 * col)     col 0..54
    world_y =  -96 + (16 * row)     row 0..12

The grid IS the walkable area: it stops where the white part of dream.bmp
stops, so there is nothing outside it to stand on.

Legend:
    .   walkable tile
    @   player start          col  0, row 6  -> world (-432, 0)
    F   final NPC, skips the level
                              col 54, row 6  -> world ( 432, 0)

To place an NPC, overwrite a '.' with any other letter, and list the letter
under NPCs below with whatever it should be. Notes worth knowing while you
place them:

  - An NPC tile is solid: the player cannot walk through it, he talks to it by
    standing on an adjacent tile and facing it. So do not wall off the row 6
    path from left to right unless you want the detour.
  - The camera follows the player inside col 3..51 and row 3..9. Outside that
    the view is frozen and the player walks toward the edge of the screen,
    which is why @ and F sit at the far ends: both are reachable, both are seen
    with the map edge against the screen edge.

NPCs
----
All of them are Junior sprites; the letter on the grid is the way each one
faces. They are numbered left to right, the order the player meets them, and
that is the order of npc_definitions in include/world_map_config.h. To write
an interaction, edit the dialog array named here; two lines show per page.

  #  tile          world        faces  dialog array                       role
  1  col  3, row  4  ( -384, -32)   D     host_dialog_lines                  poker host
  2  col  6, row  8  ( -336,  32)   U     slot_host_dialog_lines             slots host
  3  col 11, row  4  ( -256, -32)   R     roulette_host_dialog_lines         roulette host
  4  col 12, row  9  ( -240,  48)   R     sports_betting_host_dialog_lines   sports betting host
  5  col 15, row  3  ( -192, -48)   L     npc_05_dialog_lines                free
  6  col 19, row 11  ( -128,  80)   R     npc_06_dialog_lines                free
  7  col 22, row  2  (  -80, -64)   U     npc_07_dialog_lines                free
  8  col 22, row  4  (  -80, -32)   R     npc_08_dialog_lines                free

The four hosts keep the minigames reachable; move those triggers onto whichever
NPCs you want once you write the real cast (game_trigger in npc_definitions).
NPC 17 is the one that leaves the map: it asks its question through the
skips_level flag rather than a game_trigger.
